# MaestrEau - Usage

You can manage all of your irrigation and crop management operations from the various MaestrEau applications.

Whether on your PC or Mac, your smartphone or your tablet, the MaestrEau applications give you all the tools to manage your operations.

The system administrator must have previously configured the Areas, Zones and operating Scenarios.  See the [Configuration - MaestrEau](../maestreau-configuration/index.md) section for explanations of the operating principles.

Your user account gives you access to the following functions:

- MaestrEau on Web:
- MaestrEau on iPhone
- MaestrEau on Android

See the sections under MaestrEau - Operations for instructions on using the various management tools.
